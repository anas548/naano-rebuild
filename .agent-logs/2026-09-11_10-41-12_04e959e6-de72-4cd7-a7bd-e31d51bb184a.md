- **Industry** — lookup table, shared between creator tagging and (implicitly) ICP/campaign matching.
- **Campaign** — belongs to Company, has a brief (derived from value prop + ICPs), status, budget.
- **Collaboration/Booking** — the join entity between Campaign and CreatorProfile; carries status (invited/applied/active/needs action/declined/completed), next action, due date, amount — this table is clearly the same underlying entity, just rendered from each side.
- **Post** — belongs to a Collaboration (or creator+campaign), LinkedIn post ref, reactions/comments/reach/qualified clicks — feeds both Results (brand) and Analytics (creator).
